Luxe Backwater Odyssey

Kerala Backwaters

Kerala Backwaters weave tranquil waterways, swaying coconut palms, and timeless village life into one peaceful escape. Expect gentle houseboat cruises by day, golden sunsets over calm lagoons, and authentic Kerala hospitality at every turn.

Package highlights

What's included

  • Private houseboat stay in Alleppey
  • Munnar tea garden day trip
  • Traditional Kerala Sadya lunch onboard
  • Kathakali dance performance evening
  • All meals included on the houseboat

Itinerary

4 Days / 3 Nights

Day 1

Arrival in Kochi

Private transfer to your hotel, followed by an evening Kathakali dance performance showcasing Kerala's traditional art form.

  • Airport Pick-up: Private transfer from Kochi airport to your hotel.
  • Hotel Check-in: Settle in and relax after your journey.
  • Kathakali Performance: Evening cultural show featuring traditional Kerala dance.
  • Overnight Stay: Rest at your Kochi hotel.

Day 2

Munnar Tea Trail

Drive to Munnar, visiting scenic tea gardens and viewpoints along the way, before settling in for the night amid the hills.

  • Transfer to Munnar: Scenic drive through Kerala's hill country.
  • Tea Garden Visit: Explore sprawling tea plantations and learn about tea processing.
  • Viewpoints: Stop at scenic lookout points en route.
  • Overnight Stay: Rest at your Munnar hotel.

Day 3

Alleppey Houseboat

Board your private houseboat for an overnight backwater cruise, complete with a traditional Kerala Sadya lunch onboard.

  • Transfer to Alleppey: Travel to the houseboat departure point.
  • Houseboat Boarding: Begin your private cruise through the tranquil backwaters.
  • Kerala Sadya Lunch: Enjoy a traditional multi-course meal onboard.
  • Overnight on Houseboat: Sleep aboard as you drift through the calm lagoons.

Day 4

Departure

Disembark after breakfast and transfer to Kochi airport for your onward journey.
