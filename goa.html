Itinerary

3 Days / 2 Nights

Day 1

Arrival & North Goa Exploration

Your first day in Goa offers a perfect mix of exploration, relaxation, and excitement. From visiting iconic landmarks like Chapora Fort with its breathtaking sea views to experiencing the laid-back charm of Anjuna and Vagator beaches, the day is filled with scenic beauty and cultural experiences. After enjoying a delicious Goan lunch, unwind at your resort before stepping into the vibrant nightlife of Calangute or Baga Beach.

  • Arrival at Madgaon Railway Station: Reach Madgaon and begin your journey.
  • Depart to North Goa: Travel towards North Goa and enjoy the scenic drive.
  • Check-in: Check-in depending on arrival time and freshen up.
  • Explore North Goa's Best Spots: Visit Chapora Fort, Anjuna Beach (Hippies Beach), and Vagator Beach.
  • Lunch: Enjoy a delicious Goan meal at a local restaurant.
  • Resort Activities: Relax at the resort, enjoy pool time, or participate in resort activities.
  • Night Walk to Party Beaches: Stroll along Calangute Beach or Baga Beach, famous for nightlife and parties.
  • Dinner & Overnight Stay: Enjoy dinner and rest for the night.

Day 2

Leisure & Relaxation

Start your day with a delicious breakfast at the hotel, then enjoy the day at your own pace — relax on the beach, indulge in optional activities, and end with a peaceful sunset walk along the shore.

  • Breakfast at Hotel: Start your day with a delicious breakfast.
  • Leisure Day: Enjoy the day at your own pace, exploring Goa as you wish.
  • Relax at the Beach or Resort: Soak up the sun or unwind with resort amenities like the pool and spa.
  • Optional Activities: Indulge in water sports, explore local markets, or visit nearby cafes.
  • Sunset by the Beach: End the day with a peaceful evening walk along the shore.
  • Dinner & Overnight Stay: Return to the hotel for dinner and a restful night.

Day 3

Exploring Goa's Heritage & Beaches

End your Goa trip with a perfect blend of history, culture, and beachside relaxation, taking home unforgettable memories.

  • Breakfast: Start your day with a nutritious breakfast at the hotel.
  • Visit Aguada Fort: Explore the historic 17th-century fort, known for its stunning views of the Arabian Sea.
  • Old Goa Exploration: Visit St. Augustine Tower, Old Goa Church, and the Basilica of Bom Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Adventure River Rafting (Optional): Experience river rafting on the Beas River (additional cost).
  • Lunch Break: Enjoy a delightful meal at a local Goan restaurant.
  • Colva Beach Visit: Relax at Colva Beach, known for its golden sands and serene ambiance.
  • Shopping Time: Browse local markets for souvenirs and Goan specialties.
  • Dinner & Departure: Savor your final dinner in Goa before heading to Madgaon Railway Station for departure.
