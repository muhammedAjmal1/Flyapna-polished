Our Story

Born from a love of real travel, not checkout forms

FlyApna started because booking a holiday shouldn't feel like filling out a tax form. We wanted travel planning to feel like it did before — a real conversation with someone who actually knows the place you're going.

Today we're a small team who plan every trip personally over a call or WhatsApp — no call centres, no templated packages, just honest advice and pricing.

From weekend Kerala backwater escapes to full Maldives honeymoons, every FlyApna itinerary is shaped around the person taking it — never copy-pasted.
