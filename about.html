About FlyApna

Travel planning, the human way

We're a small team that plans trips the way we'd plan one for a friend — over a call, not a checkout form.

Our story

Why FlyApna exists

FlyApna started with a simple frustration: booking a holiday online usually means twelve browser tabs, no one to actually ask questions to, and a price that changes by the time you decide. We wanted trip planning to feel more like calling a well-travelled friend.

Today, we help travellers across Kerala and beyond put together flights, hotels, and holiday packages — with a real person on WhatsApp at every step, from the first "where should we go?" to the airport pickup.

What we believe

Honest pricing. No hidden charges revealed at checkout.

Real availability. We confirm before we quote, not after.

A human on the other end. Every trip has one point of contact, start to finish.
