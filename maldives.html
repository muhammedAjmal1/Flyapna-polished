Maldives

Itinerary

4 Days / 3 Nights

Day 1

Arrival and Check-in

Upon arrival at Malé International Airport, our transportation team will ensure a smooth transfer to Maafushi Island. Enjoy a warm welcome and spend the day exploring the island's scenic beauty. Later, embark on a memorable sunset cruise with a fishing experience, followed by a delicious fish BBQ dinner.

  • Explore Maafushi Island: Visit Bikini Beach, Floating Bar, and other attractions.
  • Sunset Cruise & Fishing: Enjoy a scenic evening on the water.
  • Fish BBQ Dinner: Savor a freshly prepared island dinner.
  • Accommodation: Hotel in Maafushi (Stay – Night 1).

Day 2

Half-Day Snorkeling & Sandbank Adventure

After breakfast, set out on an exciting half-day trip filled with marine adventures. Begin with a thrilling snorkeling experience among vibrant coral reefs, continue to the lucky dolphin and turtle reef, then relax on a pristine sandbank surrounded by turquoise waters.

  • Snorkeling: Explore vibrant coral reefs and colorful marine life.
  • Dolphin & Turtle Reef: Spot dolphins and sea turtles in their natural habitat.
  • Sandbank Relaxation: Unwind on a breathtaking sandbank.
  • Lunch: Enjoy a packed lunch on the sandbank.
  • Accommodation: Return to the island and unwind at your hotel (Stay – Night 2).

Day 3

Explore the Maldives & Exciting Adventures

The Maldives is home to a vast population of reef manta rays and an annual migration of oceanic manta rays. Take this day to explore the wonders of the ocean or indulge in thrilling adventure activities at your own pace.

  • Manta Ray Encounter: Witness graceful manta rays in their natural habitat.
  • Shark Trip: Experience an up-close encounter with reef sharks.
  • Island Hopping: Discover the beauty of nearby islands.
  • Private Island Resort Tour (Optional): Indulge in luxury at an exclusive resort.
  • Adventure Activities: Parasailing, jet skiing, fun tubing, kayaking, and more.
  • Accommodation: Overnight stay at your hotel (Stay – Night 3).

Day 4

Departure

On your final day, enjoy a delicious breakfast before checking out from the hotel. Based on your flight time, you will be transferred to Velana International Airport by speedboat, ensuring a smooth and scenic journey home.
